Why it converts

A property site has exactly one job: get the enquiry.

Buyers above a few million don't fill in forms because a site is pretty. They enquire when the site removes doubt — about the property, the price, and whether the person on the other end is a professional. Every decision in this build is aimed at removing one of those doubts.

Straight answers

The questions dealers actually ask

Why would I pay this when a template is $80? +

You wouldn't, if a website were decoration. A template gives you someone else's structure, the same layout as four competitors in your postcode, and no thinking about where your enquiry comes from. What you are buying here is the difference between a site that describes your business and one that produces instructions. On a $200,000 commission, that difference does not need to be large to be worth it.

Can it pull listings from my MLS / portal feed? +

Yes — that is the Bespoke tier. Every listing in the demo already renders from a single structured data source, so replacing that source with an IDX/MLS feed, a CMS, or an Airtable base is a plumbing job, not a redesign. If your feed is available I will test against it before quoting.

Who updates it after launch? +

Your team, in about two minutes per listing, using a CMS with plain fields — photos, price, beds, description. You get a recorded walkthrough. If you would rather never see an admin screen, the care plan or per-listing loading covers it.

What if I already have a site I don't hate? +

Then we don't rebuild it. I will happily quote for just the pieces that move the numbers — usually the listing detail page and a valuation funnel — and fit them to your existing design. Not every conversation needs to end in a full project.

Do you work with agents outside your own market? +

Yes. The demo is set in coastal California because the price points make the design decisions visible, but the same structure works for developers, commercial brokers, holiday-let portfolios and land dealers. The currency, units and legal footers all change with the market.

How do I know the leads are real? +

Analytics and form tracking are configured at launch, so from day one you can see which page produced each enquiry. If the valuation funnel is generating nothing in month two, we will know, and I would rather fix it than defend it.
