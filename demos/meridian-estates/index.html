The practice

A brokerage that behaves more like a private office.

Meridian was founded in 2009 on a narrow premise: that the owner of a $20 million house does not want a marketing campaign. They want one person accountable, a shortlist of genuinely qualified buyers, and no unnecessary attention.

Seventeen years later we still cap the book at sixty active representations, and every one of them is led personally by a partner.
